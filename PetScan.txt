AI Decision Layer
The platform should not rely on a single modality. Instead, combine outputs into an integrated health assessment:
1. Image AI (thermal, structural, ultrasound)
2. Motion AI (gait and posture)
3. Signal AI (ECG, heart sounds)
4. Laboratory AI (blood biomarkers)
5. Clinical fusion engine
The fusion engine generates:
* Organ-specific health scores (heart, liver, kidneys, skin, musculoskeletal)
* Disease-specific risk scores (e.g., osteoarthritis, dermatitis, DCM)
* Confidence levels based on agreement between modalities
* Recommended next steps, such as monitoring, further imaging (X-ray, MRI, CT), biopsy, or specialist referral

## Additional hardware I'd recommend

To make the platform more comprehensive, consider adding:

* **3D LiDAR scanner** for precise body geometry and volumetric measurements.
* **Force plates** integrated into the examination table or gait walkway to objectively measure limb loading.
* **RFID/microchip reader** for automatic patient identification.
* **Environmental sensors** (ambient temperature, humidity, and lighting) to improve thermal imaging consistency.
* **High-fidelity microphones** to record coughing, breathing sounds, and vocalizations for future AI analysis.
* **Automated probe and camera positioning system** to improve repeatability between examinations.

Together, these components form a complete hardware ecosystem that supports a multimodal, non-sedated veterinary assessment platform rather than a collection of standalone diagnostic devices.
