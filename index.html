How it works

From intake to a vet-signed report, in four steps.

01

Register or book a visit

Create an owner account, or have your clinic add your pet's profile in seconds.

02

Six-modality hardware scan

Thermal, ultrasound, cardiac, biomarker, gait, and structural sensors capture data in one non-sedated session.

03

AI fusion analysis

Findings across every modality are cross-referenced into one structured clinical picture with evidence-based reasoning.

04

Vet review & sign-off

A licensed veterinarian reviews, can override any finding, and digitally signs before it reaches you.

Diagnostic modalities

Six instruments, fused into one unified clinical picture.

🌡️

Thermal Imaging

FLIR-grade thermography maps surface temperature to flag inflammation, injury, and circulatory issues.

🔊

Ultrasound

Point-of-care B-mode imaging for soft-tissue, organ, and cardiac structure assessment.

💓

Cardiac

ECG, pulse-ox, and non-invasive blood pressure captured together for a full cardiovascular snapshot.

🩸

Biomarkers

Point-of-care blood panel results feed directly into the fusion engine alongside imaging findings.

🐾

Gait Analysis

Force-plate and motion-capture data quantify asymmetry, weight-bearing, and mobility over time.

🎥

Structural

3D LiDAR body mapping tracks posture, conformation, and structural change across visits.
